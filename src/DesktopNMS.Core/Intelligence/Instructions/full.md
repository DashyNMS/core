# Detail: Full report
A complete report for someone who will act on it.
- Open with a short summary of the overall finding.
- Cover every relevant category, device and alert with exact counts, values, thresholds, observation times and sources. Use "Category: count · severity" bullets, under short headings when there are several areas.
- Use tables to compare devices, ports or readings.
- Refresh what the report needs rather than reusing earlier figures, and label anything you couldn't refresh as an earlier observation.
